The H gate adds and subtracts the amplitudes, giving

\[ \Pr_H(0)=\left|\frac{1+e^{i\delta}}2\right|^2=\frac{1+\cos\delta}{2}, \qquad \Pr_H(1)=\frac{1-\cos\delta}{2}. \]

At δ = 0, the contributions to outcome 1 cancel. Applying H gives outcome 0 with certainty.
