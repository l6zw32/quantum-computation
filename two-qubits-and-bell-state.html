One qubit has two amplitudes. Two qubits have four, and those four numbers can describe states that cannot be assembled from separate one-qubit states. The Bell state will be our running example of this phenomenon.

Combining two qubits

We write the two qubits in the order |q0q1⟩|q_0q_1\rangle: q0q_0 is always the leftmost bit. The computational basis is |00⟩,|01⟩,|10⟩,|11⟩|00\rangle,|01\rangle,|10\rangle,|11\rangle, represented by the four coordinate vectors (1,0,0,0)T(1,0,0,0)^T, (0,1,0,0)T(0,1,0,0)^T, (0,0,1,0)T(0,0,1,0)^T, and (0,0,0,1)T(0,0,0,1)^T. Here the superscript TT turns a row into a column. A general pure state is

|ψ⟩=a|00⟩+b|01⟩+c|10⟩+d|11⟩,\begin{split}|\psi\rangle&=a|00\rangle+b|01\rangle\\&\quad+c|10\rangle+d|11\rangle,\end{split}
|a|2+|b|2+|c|2+|d|2=1.|a|^2+|b|^2+|c|^2+|d|^2=1.

Measuring both qubits gives 00,01,10,1100,01,10,11 with probabilities |a|2,|b|2,|c|2,|d|2|a|^2,|b|^2,|c|^2,|d|^2, respectively. The resulting state is the basis state observed.

If the qubits are prepared separately in α|0⟩+β|1⟩\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle and γ|0⟩+δ|1⟩\gamma|0\rangle+\delta|1\rangle, their joint state is the tensor product, denoted by ⊗\otimes. For two columns, its rule is

(αβ)⊗(γδ)=(αγαδβγβδ).\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\\\beta\end{pmatrix}\otimes \begin{pmatrix}\gamma\\\delta\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\gamma\\\alpha\delta\\\beta\gamma\\\beta\delta\end{pmatrix}.

Equivalently, expand the tensor product distributively, using |a⟩⊗|b⟩=|ab⟩|a\rangle\otimes|b\rangle=|ab\rangle. A state expressible this way is a product state. For example, |+⟩⊗|0⟩=(|00⟩+|10⟩)/2|+\rangle\otimes|0\rangle=(|00\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2, where |+⟩=(|0⟩+|1⟩)/2|+\rangle=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2. Tensor products describe joint systems, not ordinary multiplication of two column vectors. [1]

In a product state, measurement probabilities factor as expected: the probability of 1111 is |βδ|2=|β|2|δ|2|\beta\delta|^2=|\beta|^2|\delta|^2. If both input vectors are normalized, their tensor product is normalized too, because the sum of its four squared magnitudes is (|α|2+|β|2)(|γ|2+|δ|2)=1(|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2)(|\gamma|^2+|\delta|^2)=1.

Building the Bell state

For gates, A⊗BA\otimes B applies AA to the first qubit and BB to the second. On a product state it acts as (A⊗B)(|u⟩⊗|v⟩)=A|u⟩⊗B|v⟩(A\otimes B)(|u\rangle\otimes|v\rangle)=A|u\rangle\otimes B|v\rangle, and we extend this rule linearly to every state.

Start in |00⟩|00\rangle. Applying HH to q0q_0 gives

(H⊗I)|00⟩=|00⟩+|10⟩2.(H\otimes I)|00\rangle=\frac{|00\rangle+|10\rangle}{\sqrt2}.

The controlled-NOT gate, or CNOT, uses q0q_0 as its control and q1q_1 as its target. Its rule is |ab⟩↦|a,b⊕a⟩|ab\rangle\mapsto|a,b\oplus a\rangle, where ⊕\oplus is addition modulo 22. Thus it fixes 0000 and 0101, and swaps 1010 with 1111. It permutes the basis vectors, so it is unitary. By linearity,

CNOT|00⟩+|10⟩2=|00⟩+|11⟩2=|Φ+⟩.\operatorname{CNOT}\frac{|00\rangle+|10\rangle}{\sqrt2} =\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2} =|\Phi^+\rangle.

This is a Bell state. Measuring both qubits returns 0000 or 1111, each with probability 1/21/2. Measuring only q0q_0 likewise gives a fair bit; conditioned on its value, the joint state becomes |00⟩|00\rangle or |11⟩|11\rangle.

Why the Bell state is entangled

A pure state of two qubits is entangled if it is not a product state. Suppose |Φ+⟩|\Phi^+\rangle were the product above. Comparing coefficients would require

αγ=βδ=1/2,αδ=βγ=0.\alpha\gamma=\beta\delta=1/\sqrt2, \qquad \alpha\delta=\beta\gamma=0.

The first two equalities make all four factors nonzero, contradicting the other equalities. Therefore this state is entangled. Correlated outcomes alone would not prove this: choosing 0000 or 1111 using a classical coin gives the same computational-basis probabilities. The coefficient argument concerns the quantum state itself.

For nn qubits, the same rules give |ψ⟩=∑x∈{0,1}nαx|x⟩|\psi\rangle=\sum_{x\in\{0,1\}^n}\alpha_x|x\rangle, with ∑x|αx|2=1\sum_x|\alpha_x|^2=1. Listing every amplitude now uses 2n2^n entries. We will look for a shorter description of a useful family of these states.

The first gate produces (|00⟩+|01⟩)/2(|00\rangle+|01\rangle)/\sqrt2. CNOT fixes both terms because the control is 00. The result is |0⟩⊗|+⟩|0\rangle\otimes|+\rangle, a product state.
